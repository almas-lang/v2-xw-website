The widening gap

The AI gap is widening every week. Which side are you on?

The faster half

Most designers are pouring their energy into the wrong half of AI. They're getting faster at making the same deliverables — and quietly turning themselves into the most replaceable person on the team.

The senior half

Meanwhile, a smaller group of designers figured out the other half. They're using AI to design experiences that weren't even possible two years ago. They're the ones getting pulled into strategy rooms, handed the senior title, and paid like it.

The gap between these two groups isn't talent. It isn't years of experience. It's one shift in how you see AI — and it compounds. Every month you wait, the designers who made the shift pull further ahead, and the climb back gets steeper.

This session is where you cross to the right side of that gap. In 90 minutes. For free. Once.
